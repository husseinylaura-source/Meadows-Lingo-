Meadows Lingo · Teacher tool

Record your voice for the listening parts

Record each text in your own voice. Students then hear you instead of the computer voice. Recordings are kept in this browser until you download them, so you can do a few at a time.

Back to the dashboard
1. RecordPress the red button, read the text, press it again to stop. Play it back and re-record if you like.
2. DownloadPress Download recordings. You get one .zip file.
3. Upload to GitHubUnzip it. In your repository open audio/voice, choose Add file → Upload files, drag in everything from the zip (including manifest.json) and commit.
